Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send the contact form, we receive the details you enter: your name, business email, company, company size, main bottleneck, any details you add and, if you used it, your calculator estimate. We also record the page address, the referring page and campaign tags (UTM parameters) so we know how you found us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange a consultation or audit.
  • To prepare for that conversation.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns bring enquiries.

We don’t sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Analytics and cookies

If analytics is enabled, we ask for your consent first. Analytics cookies are set only after you accept them. You can decline and the site works the same.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting engagement, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@improfact.com.
